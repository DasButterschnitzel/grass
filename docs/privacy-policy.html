Zen Mower – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Zen Mower (package com.butterschnitzel.zenmower, “the app”) processes. The app is published by [DEVELOPER NAME] (“we”). Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. The app has no user accounts, no login and no own servers. Your game progress (level, coins, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device. If you use Android backup, the operating system may back this data up to your Google account under Google’s terms.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC. To deliver, measure and – with your consent – personalise ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android settings),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics/crash data of the SDK.

Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions)

Where required by law (e.g. GDPR / ePrivacy), the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (a certified consent management platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Without consent you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

Legal bases: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for showing non-personalised ads that finance the free game.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing and to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we do not store personal data ourselves, requests concerning ad data are best directed to Google; you can also contact us at the address above.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.
